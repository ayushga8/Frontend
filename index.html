Pro advice

Smart Tech Tips

Make the most out of your devices

🔒 Keep Your Data Safe

Use strong passwords, enable 2-factor authentication, and never reuse the same password across accounts.

⚡ Speed Up Your PC

Disable startup programs, clear temp files regularly, and keep your drivers and OS updated.

🔋 Extend Battery Life

Lower screen brightness, turn off Bluetooth when not in use, and avoid charging to 100% every time.

☁️ Use Cloud Backup

Back up files to Google Drive or OneDrive. Never lose your college assignments or projects again.

🛡️ Keep Software Updated

Install OS and app updates regularly. Most security issues are patched through updates — don't skip them.

📶 Secure Your Wi-Fi

Use WPA3 encryption, change your router's default password, and avoid sensitive work on public Wi-Fi.
