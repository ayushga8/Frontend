Smart Tech Tips

Make the most out of your devices

🔒 Keep Your Data Safe

Use strong passwords, enable 2-factor authentication, and never reuse the same password across accounts.

⚡ Speed Up Your PC

Disable startup programs, clear temp files regularly, and keep your drivers and OS updated.

🔋 Extend Battery Life

Lower screen brightness, turn off Bluetooth/Wi-Fi when not in use, and avoid charging to 100% every time.

☁️ Use Cloud Backup

Back up important files to Google Drive or OneDrive. Never lose your college assignments or projects again.

🛡️ Keep Software Updated

Install OS and app updates regularly. Most security vulnerabilities are patched through updates — don't skip them.

📶 Secure Your Wi-Fi

Use WPA3 encryption on your router, change the default password, and avoid doing sensitive work on public Wi-Fi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common tech questions answered

RAM (Random Access Memory) is temporary storage used while your device is running — it gets cleared when you switch off. ROM (Read Only Memory) stores permanent data like the OS that stays even after shutdown.

For most BTech students, a mid-range laptop (Core i5 / Ryzen 5, 16GB RAM, SSD) is more than enough. Gaming laptops are heavier and have poor battery life. Only go for gaming if your course needs heavy graphics work.

5G coverage in India is still expanding but it's a good idea to future-proof your phone with a 5G-ready model. Cities like Jaipur already have growing 5G coverage, so you'll benefit from it soon enough.

Delete temp files, uninstall apps you don't use, empty the recycle bin, and move large files (videos, old projects) to an external drive or cloud. On Windows, use "Storage Sense" for automatic cleanup.
